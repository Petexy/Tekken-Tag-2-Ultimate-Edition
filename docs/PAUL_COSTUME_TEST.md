# Paul Phoenix costume pages (Windows)

Paul is part of the [native roster costume importer](NATIVE_COSTUME_ROSTER.md).
Open **Start Costume Test.cmd**, then **Native roster · Tekken 7 costumes →
Choose presets…**, select **Paul Phoenix**, and choose outfits.

His ten offered Tekken 7 presets use two imported pages (six and four entries).
Each page keeps his three original costumes and all five customisation slots.
In CHANGE COSTUME, Up/Down changes pages; Left/Right selects an entry.
Confirm/cancel retain their usual buttons.

The roster-capable runtime accepts existing Paul packs. His original body codes
and reserved texture IDs remain stable. The extended importer still keeps Paul's
native identity, moveset, voice and roster cell.

Model appearance remains experimental. The known shading and ninja hair/head
layering issues are deferred. See the roster documentation for installation,
cache, backup, validation and other supported fighters.
